How to run a live test

  • Open the Run tab.
  • Confirm the protocol (change it on the Tests tab if needed).
  • Pick a team and mark anyone Absent.
  • Tap Start Ultra Beep.
  • When an athlete maxes out, tap their name — their level.shuttle is saved.
  • Use Undo last if you tapped the wrong person.

Protocols

Default is the classic 20 m multi-stage fitness test. You can also choose 15 m, 10 m junior, PACER, and Yo-Yo IR1 / IR2 / IE1. Tap the info button on a protocol for setup and rules.

Common questions

No sound / weak beeps?
Turn the iPhone silent switch off. On the live HUD, check that mute is off. Raise device volume. The Simulator is quieter than a real phone.

Progress disappeared?
Teams and results are stored on this device. Deleting the app removes local data. Restoring from an iCloud / device backup may restore it if Apple backed up the app container.

Standards charts?
Educational reference bands for military, police, athletes, and general fitness by age and sex. They are not official entry requirements of any organization.

CSV export?
Use the Export tab (or share from a team). Open the file in Excel, Numbers, or Google Sheets.

Something broken?
Email jnovikow@gmail.com with your iPhone model, iOS version, and a short description. Screenshots help.
